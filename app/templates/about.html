Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate a token?

Simply login to your account, select the desired department, review the estimated wait time, and click "Generate Token". Your token will be assigned instantly.

Can I cancel my token?

Yes, you can cancel your token up to 30 minutes before your estimated arrival time from your Dashboard or My Tokens page.

How long is my token valid?

Your token is valid only for the date of generation. You must visit the hospital within your estimated time window. Tokens expire at the end of the day.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all your personal information is securely encrypted and stored. We comply with all healthcare data protection regulations and never share your data with third parties.
